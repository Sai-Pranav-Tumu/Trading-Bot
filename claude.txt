Please review this Phase 2 audit and dev-fold result for an evolutionary crypto trading bot. We are trying hard to avoid lookahead bias and overfitting.

Important constraints:
- Final folds remain sealed.
- Final mode was not run.
- Phase 2 was run once on development folds only after pre-registration.
- No tuning after seeing results.
- Primary metric was pre-registered as top-25 ensemble net return.
- Pass rule: top-25 ensemble must beat cash and same-exposure passive in at least 6 of 7 development folds.
- Champion and population median are context only, not decisive.
- A real feature only counts as a pass if it also clearly beats its shuffled control.

Frozen rules:
- Price row mapping: Binance daily candle date D opens at D 00:00 UTC and closes at D+1 00:00 UTC. For a price row labeled D, external-data decision time is T = D+1 00:00 UTC.
- External data safety rule: only use rows with available_at_utc <= T - 1 hour.
- Fear & Greed availability: available_at_utc = value_date_utc + 1 day.
- Fear & Greed feature: latest usable value / 100, max age 3 days measured from value_date_utc to safety cutoff. If too old or missing, raw neutral 0.5 before standardization.
- Funding feature: mean Binance funding settlements in the 24h window ending at T - 1 hour. Require at least 2 settlements, otherwise raw neutral 0.0.
- Standardization uses historical training data only inside each fold.
- Baseline uses the same feature columns; external placeholder is constant raw 0.
- Shuffled controls use a fixed-seed shuffle of days, same shuffled day order for all coins.
- Evolution seeds/settings were identical: seeds 1..3, 60 generations, 8 windows, 9 folds with latest 2 sealed.
- Shuffled control seeds: 101, 202, 303, 404, 505.
